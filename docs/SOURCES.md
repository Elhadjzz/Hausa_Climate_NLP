# Authoritative Source Families Used in HESTC-10K

The complete machine-readable source registry is stored in `data/HESTC10K_source_catalogue.csv`.

## Domain 1 — Climate, Meteorology & Atmospheric Science

Principal source families include:

- World Meteorological Organization (WMO)
- Intergovernmental Panel on Climate Change (IPCC)
- United Nations Framework Convention on Climate Change (UNFCCC)
- NOAA / National Weather Service
- ECMWF and Copernicus
- EUMETSAT NWP SAF
- WMO/ETCCDI
- FAO

These resources support meteorology, cloud physics, atmospheric observations, climate extremes, forecasting, climate modelling and climate-policy terminology.

## Domain 2 — Water, Hydrology & Sanitation

Principal source families include:

- WHO
- WMO/UNESCO
- UNESCO-IHP
- FAO AQUASTAT
- UNEP GEMS/Water
- IAEA
- UN-Water
- UNECE
- WHO/UNICEF JMP

Coverage includes hydrology, hydrometry, groundwater, hydrochemistry, drinking water, sanitation, wastewater, irrigation, transboundary water and SDG 6.

## Domain 3 — Land, Soil, Drought & Desertification

## Source-selection criteria

A resource was preferred when it was institutionally authoritative, scientifically or legally relevant, sufficiently stable for traceability, directly related to environmental terminology, and appropriate for linking an HESTC concept to an official source.

The source catalogue is the definitive source inventory; this document is a high-level summary.
